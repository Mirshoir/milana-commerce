## English description

Explore Milana Premium clothing for women, men, kids and families. View product
photos and videos, save favorites across signed-in devices, and order by bag or
pack using Milana's wholesale quantities.

The catalog is managed by Milana Premium through the official website. After
you submit an order, a manager contacts you to confirm availability, delivery
and payment. No payment card is charged inside the app.

The app supports Uzbek, Russian and English, email recovery, Google and Apple
account access, order history, customer support, and in-app account deletion.

## Review notes

Milana Premium is a customer-facing commerce app. Product/customer/order
administration is performed only in the website admin panel and is not exposed
in the mobile app.

No digital content or in-app purchase is sold. Orders are for physical clothing,
and payment is confirmed outside the app by a Milana manager. Provide App Review
with a working test customer account and explain any regional catalog
restrictions in the submission notes.
