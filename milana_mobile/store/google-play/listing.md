# Google Play Listing Copy

## English

**App name:** Milana Premium

**Short description:** Premium clothing, saved favorites and direct wholesale ordering.

**Full description:**

Discover Milana Premium collections for women, men, kids and families. Browse
product photos and videos, save favorite pieces across signed-in devices, and
order by bag or pack using Milana's wholesale quantities.

The app connects directly to Milana Premium's current catalog. Product,
availability and order information is managed by the Milana team through the
official website. Place an order with your delivery details and a manager will
contact you to confirm stock, delivery and payment.

Features include:

- Current Milana Premium product catalog
- Product photos and videos
- Bag and pack ordering
- Saved items synced to your account
- Email, Google and Apple account access
- Order history and customer support
- Uzbek, Russian and English
- In-app account deletion

No payment card is charged inside the app.

## Russian

**Название:** Milana Premium

**Краткое описание:** Премиальная одежда, избранное и прямые оптовые заказы.

**Полное описание:**

Откройте коллекции Milana Premium для женщин, мужчин, детей и всей семьи.
