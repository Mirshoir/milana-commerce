Terms & Conditions

These operating terms are a practical draft for the Milana Premium website and mobile apps. Final public wording should be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before launch.

Company & Platform

Milana Premium sells clothing through website, mobile app, and manager-assisted channels. Product availability, final payment method, cargo route, and dispatch timing are confirmed by a manager before shipment.

Business & Individual Accounts

Registration is intentionally simple. Business and individual customers provide the same basic contact information. The difference is order type: business customers buy larger wholesale qop quantities, while individual customers may buy retail-enabled products by piece.

Orders, Payment & Delivery

Wholesale qop orders use the Milana rule: 1 qop equals 60 clothes, typically 6 sizes by 10 pieces. Retail orders use piece quantity. Online payment integration is not final; orders are accepted with pending payment status until manager confirmation.

Reviews & Moderation

Only verified purchasers may submit reviews. Reviews can include rating, comment, and optional photo, and are published after moderation.

Data & Support

Account, order, wishlist, review, and support-chat data are used to process orders and improve customer service. Customers can request help through the support page, chat widget, WhatsApp, or Telegram.
