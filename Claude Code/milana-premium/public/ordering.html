OrdersProcess

How Ordering Works

01

Choose account type

Business accounts order larger wholesale qop quantities. Individual accounts order retail-enabled items by piece.

02

Add products

Wholesale qop is calculated as 60 clothes with the size mix rule. Retail orders use piece quantity.

03

Manager confirmation

Stock, exact payment, and delivery route are confirmed by a Milana manager before dispatch.

04

Payment & shipping

Payment is currently manager-confirmed. Regional delivery time is usually 1-5 business days after dispatch, depending on cargo route.

05

Track status

Your account order history shows new, processing, shipped, done, or cancelled status. Tracking number can be added by admin.
